## I. Gather Supplies

### Basic items:
- Center well dish (`dish set`)
    - Components:
        - `center well`: inner well of `dish set`; where the gel will be cast
        - `dish bottom`: bottom piece of `dish set`
        - `dish cover`: top piece of `dish set`

- `Styrofoam bucket` for ice
- `Lab film` (Parafilm M)
- `pH test strips`
- `Eppendorf tube` (2mL)
---
### Collagen Reagents:
- `10X PBS`
- `1X NaOH`
- `DI Water`
- `Stock Collagen Solution` (Corning® Collagen I (High Concentration, Rat Tail))

---
### Supplemental Supplies:

**If treating `center well` for adhesion:**  

- Poly(ethyleneimine) (`PEI`) 
- Glutaraldehyde (`GA`)

- `Coverslip` (18 mm Circle, No. 2)

## II.  Making `Neutralized Collagen Solution`:  

### Planning & Measuring Collagen Solution:

**IMPORTANT:** If you are making anything other than 0.5 mL, 6% `gel`, follow official Corning® protocol.

> **NOTE:** Use the measurements below **for 0.5 mL, 6% gel ONLY:**  
> 
>   - 50 µL `10X PBS`  
>   - 5 µL `1X NaOH`  
>   - 151 µL `DI Water`  
>   - 292 µL `Stock Collagen Solution`  
---

### Synthesizing Collagen Solution:  
1. Fill `Styrofoam bucket` with ice

1. Place all collagen reagents on ice for 20 minutes

1. If `center well` treatment **REQUIRED**:  _Follow **`Treating Center Well`** Protocol below._

1. Pipette `10X PBS` into `Eppendorf tube`

1. Pipette `1X NaOH` into `Eppendorf tube`  
>_NOTE: From my experience, it is best if you start lower than pre-determined volume and add a little more later on if `pH` is too low._

1. Pipette `DI Water` into `Eppendorf tube`

1. Using `blue (1000µL) pipette`, cut pipette tip ~3mm from the end  
>_NOTE: Collagen solution is thick and highly viscous - you'll need larger inlet area._

1. Pipette `stock collagen solution` into `Eppendorf tube`

1. Scrape any `stock collagen solution` that remains on pipette tip back into the `stock collagen solution` container
>_NOTE: Collagen is expensive; this helps reduce transfer loss._

1. With a free yellow pipette tip, mix the `neutralized collagen solution` thoroughly in the `Eppendorf tube` for 5 wall-clock minutes, trying not to make bubbles
> _NOTE: If bubbles are introduced, allow solution to sit on ice to dissipate before casting._

1. Test `pH` on `pH test strip`: if `pH` is too low, add 1 µL `1X NaOH` at a time until `pH` is in target range
> _NOTE: For ablation testing, aim for 6.5 ≤ `pH` < 8. For cell culture, aim for exact `pH` described in protocol._

---

## III. Casting and Polymerizing `Gel`  

1. Cut new pipette tip (similarly as mentioned earlier), and pipette desired volume of `neutralized collagen solution` into `well dish`
> _NOTE: If bubbles are introduced, pop them with a fresh hypodermic needle._


1. Once satisfied with `pH`, label and date the `dish cover`, then place `dish cover` on `well dish` and wrap `lab film` tightly around the circumference of the `dish set`
>_NOTE: This is to make sure the `dish set` is airtight during polymerization in the incubator._

1. Incubate wrapped `dish set` at 37.0˚C for 60 minutes
> _NOTE: Inspect the gel after incubation to ensure it is opaque; if transparent, incubate longer._

1. Storage depends on whether a `coverslip` is required:
    - IF `COVERSLIP` **REQUIRED:** _Follow **`Applying Coverslips`** Protocol below._
    - IF `COVERSLIP` **NOT** REQUIRED: To prevent 'gel' from drying out, pipette ~50 µL `?X PBS` on top of the polymerized gel (use same osmolarity PBS as gel environment).


1. Store wrapped `dish set` in the refrigerator, noting any bubbles if present.

## IV.  Supplemental Protocols:  


### Treating `Center Well`:  
Treating the `center well` with `PEI`/`GA` may improve gel adhesion to the base and edges of the `center well`.
> _NOTE: `PEI` and `GA` evaporate rather quickly; replace green cap of conical tube promptly._

**Treatment:**  

1. Pipette ~15 µL of `PEI` into the `center well`, making sure to spread the liquid so that it makes contact with the base and walls of the `center well`. 
1. Wait ~10 minutes to dry (you may use `compressed air canister` if impatient)
1. Pipette ~15 µL of `GA` into the `center well` in the same manner. 
1. Wait until dry. 

---

### Applying `Coverslips`:  
_Adapted from Tresa Elias's Protocol_

If the coverslip is required (varies between experiments), you must place prepare it before placing it. It is important to coat `coverslip` with RainX to prevent unwanted adhesion of `coverslip` to `gel`.

**`Coverslip` Preparation:**  

1. Clean `coverslips` with `EtOH` and dry
1. Place desired number of `coverslips` on a `Kim Wipe`
1. Gently spray coverslips with `RainX`, then dab the surface with a `Kim Wipe`
1. Repeat once more and let `coverslips` completely air dry


**`Coverslip` Placement:**  

1. Gently place coverslip on top of `gel`  
1. Replace `dish cover` and re-wrap `dish set` with `lab film`
